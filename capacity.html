Capacity fade

Discharge capacity at each periodic check-up, for the 228 cells of the aging experiment. Cells aged under the same condition share a color and line style (three cells per condition), so their spread shows cell-to-cell variation. Diamonds mark where a cell falls below 80% of its reference capacity, which is the cell's own first capacity by default. Capacity is shown in Ah in both views.

Notes on the data
  • Capacity is the dataset's estimate (cap_aged_est_Ah) from the discharge at each room-temperature check-up. The check-ups themselves age the cells.
  • Check-up numbers run from CU 1 to CU 28, plus CU 29, a manual check-up performed at room temperature with air cooling. CU 29 is drawn but excluded from the 80% point, and marked “manual” in the tooltip.
  • 80% point: the first discharge capacity below 80% of a reference capacity, linearly interpolated between the two neighboring check-ups. Cells that never drop below it within the data have no marker. CU 29 is excluded.
  • 80% reference: by default each cell is measured against its own first discharge capacity (CU 1 for every cell), so the crossing reflects how fast that cell fades; each cell's threshold is then 80% of its own first capacity, a slightly different height for every cell, so the chart has no single threshold line and the diamonds sit at those heights. The alternative is the 3.0 Ah nominal capacity (a fixed 2.4 Ah line), the reference of the state-of-health scale defined in the dataset authors' paper (the 80% threshold on that reference is this project's choice, not the authors'). Cells start slightly below 3.0 Ah, so against their own capacity they cross later, and a few cells reach 2.4 Ah without reaching 80% of their own first capacity. The dataset's own state-of-health column is not used.
  • Equivalent full cycles (EFC) are the mean of the total charged and discharged throughput divided by 3.0 Ah, as defined in the dataset readme. Calendar-aged cells only accumulate throughput during check-ups, so time is the meaningful axis for them.
  • Capacity recovery before CU 24: one of the three cells of each calendar condition was held at 10% SoC at its aging temperature for about 2.5 days just before CU 24. Cells aged at high SoC regained part of their lost capacity, which appears as an upward step at CU 24 (for example P011-3: 2.667 Ah at CU 23, 2.688 Ah at CU 24).
  • Early shutoff: cells P012-1, P015-3 and P016-1 shut off at CU 24 and have no capacity after CU 23.
  • Voltage windows are the target charge and discharge voltages of the cycling profile, not state-of-charge ranges.
  • A check-up with a missing discharge capacity is skipped, and the line connects the neighboring check-ups.

Data: M. Luh and T. Blank, Comprehensive battery aging dataset: capacity and impedance fade measurements of a lithium-ion NMC/C-SiO cell (version 2: result data), RADAR4KIT, 2024, doi:10.35097/1969, licensed CC BY 4.0. Values shown are derived from that dataset; see the project overview.
