Battery aging explorer

Interactive views of how 228 lithium-ion cells aged over up to nearly 20 months of calendar, cyclic and driving-profile testing, built from a public dataset of periodic check-up measurements.

Views

  • Capacity fade

    Discharge capacity at every check-up, filtered by aging type, temperature and operating condition, with the 80% point of each cell.

  • Impedance

    High-frequency resistance (HFR) and the resistance from the HFR to the knee (the point of lowest phase angle) from impedance spectra against aging, plus Nyquist plots of individual spectra.

  • Circuit fits

    Equivalent-circuit fits of the impedance spectra: inductance from the high-frequency limit, then two-arc circuits with three low-frequency tail elements compared on a random sample of spectra.

About the data

The cells are LG INR18650HG2 cylindrical cells (NMC cathode, C-SiO anode). The experiment comprises 76 aging conditions with three cells each, held at 0, 10, 25 or 40 °C:

  • Calendar aging: cells rest at a fixed state of charge.
  • Cyclic aging: cells are charged and discharged continuously at set rates and voltage limits.
  • Profile aging: cells follow a driving profile derived from the WLTP Class 3b cycle.

Periodically each cell goes through a check-up: a capacity measurement at room temperature, electrochemical impedance spectroscopy at five states of charge, and current-pulse resistance measurements. The pages here show the capacity and impedance results, and the circuit fits of the spectra; the pulse measurements and the continuous cycling data are not used.

The raw result files are reduced to compact JSON by scripts/preprocess.py, which selects the check-up values, numbers the check-ups, computes equivalent full cycles, interpolates the 80% capacity point and reads the HFR and the HFR to knee resistance from the impedance spectra. scripts/fit_circuits.py then fits equivalent circuits to the spectra. The pages read only the resulting JSON files in the browser.
