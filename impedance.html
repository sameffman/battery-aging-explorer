Impedance

Resistances taken from the impedance spectra measured at each check-up. R0 is the ohmic resistance, where a spectrum crosses the real axis; R1 is the distance along the real axis from there to the knee of the spectrum, its point of lowest phase angle. One view follows them against aging, the other shows the spectra themselves. Cells aged under the same condition share a color and line style (three cells per condition).

Notes on the data
  • Spectra are used as stored in the dataset, with the authors' compensated impedance. Spectra marked invalid and points with missing values are dropped. At each check-up a spectrum is measured at five states of charge, at room temperature and at the cell's aging (operating) temperature. Values are in milliohms.
  • Room and aging temperature are told apart by the dataset's is_rt flag: 1 for a measurement at room temperature, 0 for one at operating (aging) temperature.
  • R0 is the real part where the spectrum crosses the real axis, found by linear interpolation between the two neighboring points, searched between 200 Hz and 3.3 kHz.
  • R1 is the real part at the knee of the spectrum minus R0. The knee is the point of lowest phase angle among the local minima of the phase at or below 33 Hz, and it must have at least two measured points at lower frequency. Without such a knee a spectrum has no R1 and the point is left out of the chart. The authors instead take the lowest phase angle over 0.49 to 33 Hz, which can return an end point of that range, and average over each check-up; here every spectrum is evaluated on its own.
  • Not fitted: R0 and R1 are read directly from the measured spectra. They are not parameters of a fitted equivalent circuit, and R1 is not identified with a single process.
  • Nyquist plot: the imaginary part is plotted as stored, positive for capacitive behavior, so spectra rise as the frequency falls. At the highest frequencies the points lie below the real axis. Diamonds mark R0 on the real axis and circles mark the knee. The authors report the data as most accurate between 0.5 Hz and 5 kHz, excluding 100 Hz and 208.3 Hz; no points are removed here on that basis.
  • Check-up numbers run from CU 1 to CU 28, plus CU 29, a manual check-up performed at room temperature with air cooling, marked “manual” in the tooltip.
  • Equivalent full cycles (EFC) are as on the capacity page. Calendar-aged cells only accumulate throughput during check-ups, so time is the meaningful axis for them.
